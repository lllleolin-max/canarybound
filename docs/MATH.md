# Model and proof

Fixed before observation: finite strata, nonnegative target weights summing exactly to one, alpha in (0,1), policy margins and enrollment independent of eventual outcomes. For each positive-weight stratum and arm, the outcome sequence in enrollment order is iid stationary Bernoulli with mean p_j. Dependence across streams is permitted; the proof needs only each stream's iid model. The implementation has 2 times the number of positive-weight strata streams, J. Outcome-based filtering and adaptive enrollment outside this model are rejected declarations; software cannot verify the truth of these attestations or detect omitted assignment records. The estimand is the weighted population adverse-rate difference. A causal interpretation needs a separately justified allocation design.

For fixed n, Hoeffding's bound is `P(|mean_n - p_j| > e) <= 2 exp(-2 n e²)`. Assign `delta_n = alpha/(J n(n+1))` to both tails together. With `e_n = sqrt(log(2 J n(n+1)/alpha)/(2n))`, the bound is delta_n. Since `sum_{n>=1} 1/(n(n+1)) = 1`, a countable union across J streams and all n has probability at most alpha. Therefore, with probability at least 1-alpha, every full-outcome mean interval covers its mean at every enrollment count. No extra independent-stream assumption is needed.

At any wall-clock look, observed adverse sum S and pending m out of n assigned units satisfy the deterministic complete sum `T in [S,S+m]`, even when revelation is arbitrarily outcome-dependent. Widen to `L=max(0,S/n-e_n)` and `U=min(1,(S+m)/n+e_n)`; use [0,1] if n=0. Every full-outcome interval is included, so the same simultaneous event covers all wall-clock looks, including repeated counts and permanent pending outcomes. This deterministic widening is essential; substituting completed-only denominators does not preserve the argument.

For nonnegative fixed weights, the population difference is enclosed by `sum(w*(L_canary-U_control))` and `sum(w*(U_canary-L_control))`. Dropping e gives separate deterministic finite assigned-cohort completion bounds. These finite bounds do not assert population coverage, iid outcomes or causal effects. A stream with n=0 contributes [0,1] to this extension. Empty data therefore produces the full difference support [-1,1], with no data-supported narrowing; a trivial acceptable margin of 1 can still classify that full support by the declared policy definition. Zero-weight strata remain reported and do not consume error budget.

Policy equality: U <= acceptable margin gives ACCEPTABLE; L > harmful margin gives HARMFUL; otherwise INCONCLUSIVE. Margins satisfy -1 <= acceptable <= harmful <= 1, making these labels disjoint. Outward arithmetic may conservatively leave an exact rational boundary inconclusive rather than create false certainty.

Arithmetic uses exact Fraction weights and finite ratios, then 80-digit Decimal with directed rounding. The log expression is evaluated as a sum of logs, so small alpha and large n do not create huge multiplicative floating operands. Decimal ln and sqrt are correctly rounded; their results are expanded outward. Population lower subtracts an upper radius with floor rounding; upper adds it with ceiling rounding; weighted endpoints are combined exactly as rationals before final outward conversion. Supported resource limits are part of the contract.

Primary sources checked 2026-10-03: [Howard et al., full paper, Definition 1 and Appendix J](https://arxiv.org/html/1810.08240v9) establishes simultaneous coverage and bounded-observation concentration. Its tighter mixture/stitching methods are credited; this package independently derives a conservative union bound. [Argo Rollouts official analysis documentation](https://argoproj.github.io/argo-rollouts/features/analysis/) supports delayed analysis, success/failure conditions and inconclusive runs. Neither sequential inference nor an inconclusive operational state is a novelty claim. No inference is made about undocumented competitor features.

Simulation exercises this declared model and is not a proof. The small completion oracle exhausts all binary fillings up to n=6; independent formula checks use 120-digit arithmetic and a direct product expression, rather than the production sum of logs.
